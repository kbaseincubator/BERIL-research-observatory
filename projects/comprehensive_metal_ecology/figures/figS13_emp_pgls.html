Figure S13. EMP-derived niche breadth PGLS: the metal-gene association is directionally consistent but not significant.

PGLS of per-Mb primary KO density vs Earth Microbiome Project (EMP) Levins' niche breadth (EMPO Level 2 environment categories, n = 539 genera with ≥5 EMP samples). The association is in the expected direction (β = −0.019) but does not reach significance (p = 0.099), likely reflecting the coarser EMPO environment resolution and lower sample size compared to the primary MicrobeAtlas analysis (n = 1,574 genera, >400,000 samples).

Context: The EMP result (β = −0.019, p = 0.099) is directionally consistent with the primary MicrobeAtlas association (β = −0.021, FDR q = 6.4×10⁻⁸). The lower power likely reflects the EMP's coarser environment taxonomy (EMPO Level 2: 7 categories vs MicrobeAtlas's >400,000 individual samples). Pagel's λ = 0.055 (near zero) indicates minimal residual phylogenetic signal after controlling for the predictor, suggesting different evolutionary dynamics in the EMP-represented subset. This result is treated as uninformative rather than contradictory.
