Figure 1. Metal-gene density is negatively associated with ecological niche breadth across 1,574 bacterial genera.

Each point is a genus (n = 1,574). X-axis: per-Mb primary KO density (z-scored; primary metal-gene set, 140 KOs). Y-axis: standardised Levins' niche breadth (B_std; MicrobeAtlas, >400,000 16S samples). PGLS regression line (λ = 0.757, β = −0.021, 95% CI [−0.028, −0.014], FDR q = 6.4×10⁻⁸). Colour encodes phylum; a genus with higher metal-gene investment per megabase tends to occupy a narrower ecological niche. Hover a point to see its name and values. Axis clipped at x = 5 (7 extreme outliers outside this range not visible but included in PGLS).
