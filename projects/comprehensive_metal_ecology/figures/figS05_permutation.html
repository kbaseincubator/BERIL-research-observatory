Figure S5. Predictor permutation confirms the primary association is not a numerical artefact.

Distribution of PGLS β from 1,000 permutations of the KO-density predictor (niche breadth vector held fixed, phylogeny unchanged). Red line = observed primary β = −0.0207. Empirical p = 0.0 (no permutation β ≤ −0.0207). Null distribution is centred near zero (mean = 1.2×10⁻⁵), as expected.
