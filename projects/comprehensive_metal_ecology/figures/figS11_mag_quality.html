(A) MAG Quality Distributions

Robustness Across Quality Scenarios
All quality-adjusted models recover a significant negative relationship (p < 0.01). Restriction to high-quality MAGs (≥90% complete, ≤5% contaminated; n=511) reduces sample size but preserves the effect direction and significance (β=−0.0178, p=0.0052), confirming that results are robust to MAG quality variation.
