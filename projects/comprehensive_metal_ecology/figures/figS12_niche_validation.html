Figure S12. Niche-Breadth Metric Validation: B_std Correlates With EMP and MGnify Biome Metrics

Cross-dataset validation of the MicrobeAtlas niche-breadth metric against independent surveys.
(A) MicrobeAtlas vs EMP
Spearman ρ = 0.211, p = 7.7×10⁻⁷
(B) MicrobeAtlas vs MGnify
Spearman ρ = 0.235, p = 2.4×10⁻⁸
(C) Bootstrap Stability
Median |Δ| = 0.0026
Validation Results
The niche-breadth metric (B_std) shows consistent positive correlations with independent measures of environmental distribution across EMP (n=539) and MGnify (n=549) datasets. Bootstrap resampling (100 iterations, n=1,574) confirms metric stability with median deviation of 0.0026, indicating robust parameter estimation.
