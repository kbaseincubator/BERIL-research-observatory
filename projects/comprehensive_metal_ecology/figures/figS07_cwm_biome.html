Figure S7. Community-weighted metal-gene investment is highest in aquatic and farm environments.

Community-weighted mean (CWM) of KO/Mb metal-gene density across 13 biomes (AusMicrobiome × MicrobeAtlas, n = 42,037 samples). CWM computed as Σ(relative abundance × genus KO/Mb density) / Σ(relative abundance) per sample; points show biome mean ± 1.96 SE. Reference line at grand mean (weighted by sample count). 1,561 / 1,574 PGLS genera matched to 16S genera.
Interpretation: Farm and aquatic communities have the highest metal-gene investment. Leaf-associated and plant communities rank lowest, consistent with more nutritionally restricted niches. The within-genus PGLS signal (P1) is therefore not driven by biome-level ecological structure: the correlation direction would need to be reversed within each biome to confound P1.
