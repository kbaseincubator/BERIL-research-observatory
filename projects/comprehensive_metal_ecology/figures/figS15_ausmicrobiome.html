Figure S15. P5 AusMicrobiome within-dataset sensitivity check: β = −0.052 (p = 2.2×10⁻¹⁵).

PGLS of per-Mb primary KO density vs MicrobeAtlas niche breadth, restricted to the 482 genera with AusMicrobiome MAG coverage. This is a within-dataset sensitivity check, not independent replication: both the predictor (KO density from AusMicrobiome MAGs) and response (MicrobeAtlas niche breadth) are derived from the same or closely related genera. The larger |β| vs P1 (−0.052 vs −0.021) is expected given the Proteobacteria-enriched composition of the Australian subset (Proteobacteria show stronger streamlining, Figure S9).

Why P5 is not independent replication: P1 and P5 use the same KO-density predictor on different genus subsets (global n = 1,574 vs Australian n = 482). The larger β in P5 reflects subset phylogenetic composition — Australian agricultural soils are Proteobacteria-rich, and Proteobacteria show β ≈ −0.021 in the clade-stratified analysis (Figure S9) — and may reflect additional soil-habitat streamlining not captured globally.
