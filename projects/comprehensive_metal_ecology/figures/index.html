Comprehensive Metal Ecology — Manuscript Figures

Interactive HTML figures for the manuscript: Genome streamlining is pervasive across prokaryotic functional categories, but a distinctive cofactor-versus-resistance split links metal-gene investment to ecological specialisation.
Palette validated (CVD ΔE = 74.6, all checks pass). Hover each chart for data tooltips; use "Show data table" for the accessible table view.
  • Figure 1 Primary scatter: metal-gene density vs niche breadth. 1,574 bacterial genera; canvas+SVG hybrid; coloured by phylum. PGLS regression line + 95% CI band (β = −0.021, FDR q = 6.4×10⁻⁸, λ = 0.757). Hover for genus name.
  • Figure 2 Functional landscape forest plot. PGLS β for 19 KEGG categories (BH-FDR) + P1 metal reference. Metal signal ranks 11th. 14/19 significant.
  • Figure 3 Internal functional split + permutation. (A) Forest plot: resistance null (β ≈ 0), cofactor strongest (β = −0.033). (B) Split-magnitude permutation: Δβ = 0.035, emp_p = 0.0 vs 1,000 null partitions.
  • Figure 4 Cofactor jackknife. Leave-one-KO-out for 4 cofactor KOs. All 3-KO remainders significant (β range −0.016 to −0.029, all p < 0.001). Signal is distributed.
  • Figure 5 Metal-specific uniformity. All 9 metals significant after BH-FDR (β range −0.017 to −0.027). No single element drives the signal.
  • Figure 6 Confounder dumbbell. Before/after β for 5 covariates. Genome size: 46.7% attenuation, p = 0.006 (robust). Latitude: amplifies signal. None meet the pre-specified decision rule.
  • Figure 7 AusMicrobiome + NGSA replication. P4 geochemical β for Cu and Zn: FDR q = 0.041 each (independent replication). Pb, Ni directionally consistent (NS). Co reversed. P5 reference shown as within-dataset sensitivity check only.

Supplementary Figures

  • Figure S1 Phylogenetic distribution of metal-gene density. Circular GTDB r207 cladogram (n = 2,283 bacterial genera). Inner ring: phylum colour. Outer ring: KO/Mb density (blue gradient). Metal-gene density is distributed across all major phyla — no strong phylogenetic clustering.
  • Figure S2 Genome-size distribution and confounding structure. (A) Histogram of mean genome size (n = 1,574 genera). (B) Genome size vs niche breadth. (C) Genome size vs KO density. Demonstrates per-Mb normalisation rationale.
  • Figure S3 Sensitivity analyses forest plot. 8 pre-specified checks (S1–S8): fixed λ, archaea-only, MAG threshold, raw niche metric, AU niche, N. hemisphere subset. All directionally consistent.
  • Figure S4 Named negative controls. Ribosomal proteins, amino acid biosynthesis, DNA repair vs metal-gene target. All controls significant — expected from genome-wide streamlining; internal split distinguishes metal genes.
  • Figure S5 Coreness-matched permutation null distribution. 1,000 coreness-matched null KO sets; P1 β = −0.021 vs null median = −0.018 (emp_p = 0.298). Demonstrates the primary β is not exceptional relative to pan-genome-prevalence-matched gene sets.
  • Figure S6 Functional subcategory comparison: AMR, TCS, ABC transporters. Side-by-side forest plots for 4 gene families. Resistance-null / cofactor-significant split is specific to metal genes; AMR, TCS, and most ABC subcategories are near-null or positive.
  • Figure S7 Community-weighted metal-gene investment by biome. CWM of KO/Mb density across 12 biomes (n = 42,037 samples, 1,561 genera). Farm and aquatic highest; leaf lowest. Demonstrates biome structure cannot confound the within-genus P1 signal.
  • Figure S8 Tier 1+2 KO co-occurrence heatmap. Spearman ρ between 118 KO frequencies across 1,573 genera. Resistance and Transport KOs form distinct co-occurrence blocks. Demonstrates functional modularity of the metal-gene panel.
  • Figure S9 Clade-stratified PGLS. Proteobacteria (q = 1.8×10⁻⁴) and Actinobacteria (q = 2.5×10⁻³) replicate. Firmicutes and Bacteroidetes directionally consistent, NS (low n_eff after phylo correction).
  • Figure S10 Heterogeneity test: phylum-level β estimates are statistically consistent. Funnel plot of β vs 1/SE for 4 clade-stratified estimates. Cochran's Q = 3.60 (df = 3, p = 0.309); I² = 16.6% — no significant heterogeneity.
  • Figure S11 MAG quality metrics and sensitivity. (A) Completeness and contamination distributions (n = 1,107 genera). (B) PGLS β under quality covariates and high-quality restriction (≥90% complete, ≤5% contamination). Primary β robust across all scenarios.
  • Figure S12 Niche-breadth metric validation. (A) MicrobeAtlas B_std vs EMP B_std (n = 539, ρ = 0.211, p = 7.7×10⁻⁷). (B) B_std vs MGnify biome Shannon (n = 549, ρ ≈ 0.24). (C) Bootstrap stability: median |Δ B_std| = 0.0026.
  • Figure S13 EMP niche breadth PGLS. β = −0.019, p = 0.099 (NS). Directionally consistent; power limited by coarser EMPO environment taxonomy. Compared to P1 MicrobeAtlas result.
  • Figure S14 Pfam metal-domain audit. 10/140 primary KOs have Pfam metal-clan evidence (7.1%); 130 lack it. Validates KEGG-curated approach over Pfam-based filtering.
  • Figure S15 P5 AusMicrobiome within-dataset check. β = −0.052 (p = 2.2×10⁻¹⁵, n = 482). Framed as within-dataset sensitivity, not independent replication. Compared to P1. Larger |β| explained by Proteobacteria-enriched AU subset.
  • Figure S16 P4 geochemical replication: NGSA spatial coverage. Map of ~1,315 NGSA ICP-MS sites across Australia coloured by Cu concentration. Inset histogram of per-site Cu (ppm). 200 km join radius to AusMicrobiome 16S samples → 482 genera with geochemical data.
